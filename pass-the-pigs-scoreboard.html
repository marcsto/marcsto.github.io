Pass the Pigs Scoreboard

Marc Stogaitis

Simple Score Keeping

Pass the Pigs is a classic push-your-luck dice game where players roll two small plastic pigs. Depending on how the pigs land—on their sides, feet, snouts, or leaning against each other—you score points. It's a fun, chaotic game, but keeping track of the score (and remembering all the point values) can be a bit of a hassle.

That's why I created a simple web-based scoreboard for Pass the Pigs. It handles the math, tracks player scores, and even includes the 'Pig Out' and 'Oinker' rules.

You can access the scoreboard here: Pass the Pigs Scoreboard.

Features

  • Easy Scoring: Just enter the roll score and hit add.
  • Multi-Player Support: Add as many players as you need.
  • Rules Built-in: Handles score resets for Pig Outs and Oinkers.
  • Mobile Friendly: Works great on your phone while you play.

Enjoy the game and let the scoreboard do the work!
