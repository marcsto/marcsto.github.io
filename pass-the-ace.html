OUR HOUSE RULES

Keep your card.
Or take a chance.

  1. Keep track of your cards. Your card is always face up on your phone. Cards you’ve held stay visible to you when they move to another player. Aces are low; kings are high. Suits don’t matter.
  2. Hold or swap. Play goes clockwise, starting after the dealer. Keep your card or exchange it with the next player. They have to accept…
  3. Unless they have a king. They tap “Reveal king!” and keep their card. You keep yours, and your turn ends.
  4. The dealer goes last. Hold, or cut the deck for a replacement. You must take the new card, even if it’s worse. Cut a king? The dealer wins and everyone else loses, even players holding kings!
  5. Time for the reveal. The lowest card loses. If the dealer is tied for lowest, only the dealer loses. Otherwise, everyone tied for lowest loses. Move the dealer one seat and go again.

No scores. No lives. Just one more round.
